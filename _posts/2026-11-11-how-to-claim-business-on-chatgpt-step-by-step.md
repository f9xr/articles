On November 10, 2026, OpenAI launched Businesses in ChatGPT in beta. The offer to business owners is unusually cheap: about an hour of admin work plus a few days of OpenAI review time, and in exchange you get a direct channel to correct what ChatGPT tells the 1.2 billion people who use it every week about your company.

This guide shows how to claim your business on ChatGPT step by step, from picking the right account to the identity check at the end. Until this week, no such channel existed. ChatGPT answered questions about plumbers, software tools, and product recommendations using whatever it could find on the open web, and if that data was wrong, it stayed wrong. Business Hub is OpenAI's fix.

It works much like Google Business Profile, except it feeds an AI conversation instead of a map listing. Every requirement below comes from OpenAI's [official setup documentation](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001635-setting-up-your-business-on-chatgpt){:target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"} and [launch announcement](https://openai.com/index/introducing-businesses-chatgpt/){:target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"}, both published this week.

> **Quick answer:** To claim your business on ChatGPT, sign in at chatgpt.com/business-hub, search for your business name, select the matching listing, and verify with a business phone, business email, DNS TXT record, or Shopify connection. After OpenAI approves the claim, complete a separate Persona identity check to publish profile changes, view insights, connect product feeds, or create a Business Agent.

## Why Claim Your Business on ChatGPT

Frame it as an investment decision. The cost is one hour of administration plus a few days of waiting on review. The return is control over the facts ChatGPT states about you, plus exposure data that only OpenAI can show you.

Once your claim and identity check clear, you can:

- Update your business name, website, phone, hours, logo, description, delivery policy, and return policy
- Connect a product feed with prices and stock levels, useful for retailers
- Earn the blue check that marks a profile as claimed by an authorized representative
- View Insights: mentions, website clicks, and discovery topics over a rolling 28-day window
- Publish a Business Agent, if you run an eligible U.S. online business

Now the risk side, because claiming has real limits. OpenAI's help docs are blunt: claiming does not guarantee increased visibility, gives you no direct control over ChatGPT's recommendations, and advertising does not influence its independent answers either. What you buy is accuracy, not promotion. For a business whose phone number or opening hours were wrong inside an AI answer, that alone justifies the hour.

For a business hoping to outrank competitors inside ChatGPT, this is not the tool.

## Who Can Claim a Business on ChatGPT

You must own the business or be authorized to represent it, and OpenAI verifies that connection before approving anything. You do not need an ads account or a ChatGPT Enterprise agreement to claim. Availability depends on business type and location, and the [Businesses in ChatGPT policy](https://openai.com/policies/businesses-in-chatgpt/){:target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"} governs the details:

| Business type | What you can do today |
|---------------|-----------------------|
| Online business with a website | Claim or add a profile wherever ChatGPT is available and verification is supported |
| Local store or restaurant | Claim an existing U.S. listing only |
| Ecommerce or online service (U.S.) | Claim a profile and create a Business Agent |
| Existing OpenAI Enterprise customer or advertiser | Claim through your OpenAI representative |
| Shopify merchant | Connect your store to speed up verification |

One caveat from the policy: OpenAI can decline or remove profiles in categories where its safeguards are not ready yet, and eligibility rules can change. Treat the table as the launch-day picture, not a permanent rulebook. ## How to Claim Your Business on ChatGPT, Step by Step

Work through the steps in order. The claim review and the identity check are two separate processes, and OpenAI emails its decision for each. Plan for a few days end to end.

### Step 1: Sign in with a company-controlled account

Use a ChatGPT account the company controls, not a personal login that leaves with a departing employee. Open Business Hub from the ChatGPT sidebar, or go straight to [chatgpt.com/business-hub](https://chatgpt.com/business-hub){:target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"}. The admin who claims the business can later add teammates in Business Hub and even grant an agency access, so the claim does not live or die with one person's account.

### Step 2: Find your business listing

Search for your business by name. ChatGPT often already has a profile built from public web data. Select the matching listing and confirm which account will manage it. If you cannot find your business and you run an eligible online business, you may see an option to add it.

Local U.S. stores and restaurants can only claim existing listings for now.

Two dead ends have official exits. If the listing is locked, choose Contact support. If someone else already claimed your business, open the profile, select the more options menu (three dots), and choose Dispute ownership, which routes you through Persona verification.

### Step 3: Choose a verification method

ChatGPT shows only the methods that apply to your business. Pick one:

| Method | How it works | Best for |
|--------|--------------|----------|
| Business phone | Code by SMS or automated call to the listed number | Local businesses with a staffed main line |
| Business email | Code sent to the business email address on the listing | Teams that control the listed inbox |
| Website domain | Add the TXT record ChatGPT shows to your DNS, then select Verify domain | Anyone with DNS access; the most reliable option |
| Shopify store | Connect Shopify and approve the permissions | Shopify merchants |

For the DNS route, ChatGPT displays the record's type, host, and value, and you copy each one exactly into your domain's DNS settings. Propagation can take minutes to hours. It is the same mechanism Google Search Console uses for domain verification, so most web teams have done it before.

### Step 4: Wait for claim approval

OpenAI reviews the claim and emails a decision or a request for more information. While the claim is pending you can cancel it. A pending claim is not an approved one, and nothing is usable yet.

### Step 5: Complete the Persona identity check

This step decides whether you get anything beyond the claim itself. Until Persona verification is approved, you cannot publish profile changes, view Insights, connect a product feed, or create a Business Agent.

Prepare a government-issued ID, your business registration details, and proof that you are authorized to represent the business. OpenAI is explicit about the failure mode: the business name, address, and registration or tax numbers must match your official records, or the check fails and you resubmit corrected information. OpenAI reviews the submission and emails the result, and once both approvals clear, the profile gets the blue check.

You can also postpone this step and return later: open the Business tab, select your profile, and find Complete verification with Persona.

### Step 6: Update your profile and turn on features

Edit your details so they match your website and your Google Business Profile exactly: description, logo, hours, service area, and return or delivery policies. OpenAI reviews and publishes changes, and updates take time to appear in listings and answers, because ChatGPT may also draw on other sources. Retailers can connect a product feed, and eligible U.S. online businesses can build and publish a Business Agent.

## What Insights, Feeds, and Business Agents Actually Do

### Insights measure exposure over 28 days

Insights show aggregate activity over the past 28 days, with privacy thresholds applied, so early numbers run thin. Three metrics ship at launch: mentions, website clicks, and discovery topics.

The mention definition deserves a close read. For an online business, a mention counts only when ChatGPT references the business's website; the brand name appearing alone does not count. For a local business, a mention counts when ChatGPT shows a result for that location, whether or not the customer opens the listing.

A blank metric does not mean zero activity; it can mean the reporting threshold has not been met. Judge the trend after a month, not the first reading.

### Product feeds share price and availability

Retailers can upload a CSV feed that follows OpenAI's product feed specification, or point to a hosted URL. Prices, availability, and product details then flow into what ChatGPT can tell customers. Two caveats come straight from the help docs: products must follow OpenAI's commerce policies, and connecting a feed does not guarantee ChatGPT recommends your products.

### Business Agents answer questions, not orders

Eligible U.S. ecommerce and online service businesses can publish an agent that answers customer questions using the business's own information: product differences, return policies, availability. The conversation is clearly labeled, separate from the customer's original ChatGPT chat, and the customer chooses to start it.

What agents cannot do is equally specific. They cannot complete purchases, make bookings, or check an order status; they explain and link to your website, where those actions finish. If your policy pages are vague, your agent will be vague, so the preparation work happens on your own site. ## Practical Tips for a Smooth Claim

- Use a permanent company email for the managing account, since teammate and agency access hang off the original claim
- Make sure your website is live and its details match what you submit, because domain verification and OpenAI's review both lean on it
- Keep your Google Business Profile and other listings consistent, since ChatGPT keeps using multiple sources even after you claim. Our [free business directories guide](https://f9xr.org/articles/2026/07/29/free-business-directories-guide.html) lists the ones worth fixing first
- Prepare your ID and registration documents before Step 5 so the identity check does not stall
- Check Insights monthly; the 28-day window and reporting thresholds make weekly reads meaningless
- Connect Shopify early if you sell there, because it speeds up verification and carries your catalog into ChatGPT. OpenAI's [Businesses in ChatGPT page](https://openai.com/business/businesses-in-chatgpt/){:target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"} walks through the store connection in three steps
- If you operate several brands, note that each brand is managed as a separate profile; there is no central multi-brand management yet
