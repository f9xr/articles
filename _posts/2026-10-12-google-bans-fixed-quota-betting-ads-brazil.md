Brazil spent years building a licensed betting market. A federal licence was reported to cost around R$30 million, roughly US$6 million, before an operator could serve a single customer. On September 30, 2026, Google banned fixed-quota betting ads in Brazil and switched off the advertising channel that market leaned on. No warning period, no wind-down.

Five days earlier, on September 25, Brazil's government published Provisional Measure 1.394, which prohibits operating, offering, intermediating and advertising fixed-odds betting across the country. Google's policy change is the advertising side of that decision.

You may not run a betting brand. But if you buy ads, manage campaigns for clients, publish content, or sell to Brazilian customers, this is a clean example of how fast one platform rule can turn off a growth channel and strand a budget. This guide covers what changed, what is still open, and the steps that make sense this week.

## The Quick Answer

**Did Google ban fixed-quota betting ads in Brazil?** Yes. As of September 30, 2026, Google Ads disallows the promotion of fixed quota betting in Brazil, removes the category from the permitted gambling types, and will no longer certify advertisers for it. Existing Brazil certificates for lotteries and horse racing are also revoked, and those advertisers must re-apply.

## What Exactly Did Google Change?

Google's notice makes three changes for Brazil, and it pays to separate them.

### 1. Fixed quota betting ads are disallowed

You can no longer promote this type of betting to people in Brazil through Google Ads. The wording is direct: it took effect immediately.

### 2. The category is gone from the permitted list

Fixed quota betting no longer appears among the allowed types of online gambling for Brazil, and advertisers cannot apply for certification for it. You can read the current wording on Google's [Gambling and games policy page](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/15132179){:target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"}.

### 3. Lottery and horse racing certificates are revoked

To make the change work technically, Google is cancelling all existing Brazil certificates for these two categories. Lotteries and horse racing are not banned by this notice. They are being reset, which means lost ad time for anyone who does not re-apply quickly.

### What is "fixed quota" betting?

Fixed quota betting is the English version of the Brazilian term *apostas de quota fixa*. It is the same idea most markets call fixed-odds betting: you know your potential return at the moment you place the bet. Sports betting and many online casino style games work this way, and this is the market Brazil regulated under Lei 14.790/2023.

The distinction matters for compliance teams. The ban targets one category, not gambling as a whole. Lotteries that are authorized by law fall outside the measure, and daily fantasy sports still has its own path.

## Why Google Banned Fixed-Quota Betting Ads in Brazil

Google said the update reflects recent regulatory changes. The big one is Medida Provisória 1.394, signed by President Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva and published on September 25, 2026. The [full text on Planalto](https://www.planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/_ato2023-2026/2026/mpv/mpv1394.htm){:target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"} is short, and you should read it before you accept a client's version of what it says.

Based on that text and the [Ministry of Finance explainer](https://www.gov.br/fazenda/pt-br/composicao/orgaos/secretaria-de-premios-e-apostas/medida-provisoria-ndeg-1-394-2026-entenda-as-novas-regras-para-as-apostas-de-quota-fixa){:target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"}, the measure:

- Prohibits operating, offering, intermediating and advertising fixed-odds betting in Brazil, online and physical
- Reaches offshore operators serving users located in Brazil
- Ends existing federal, state and district authorizations 30 days after publication
- Requires Congress approval within 120 days to become permanent law

The reversal is sharp. Brazil built this licensed market only recently. When Google first introduced Brazil certification rules in 2024, advertisers had to be authorized by the Ministry of Finance, and licensing was reported to cost R$30 million. That framework is now in question.

## The Legal Picture Is Still Moving

The story has not settled, and any advertiser treating it as final is guessing.

Industry groups asked Justice Luiz Fux of the Supreme Federal Court to suspend the measure, while Brazil's Attorney General's Office asked the court to keep the ban in force. Lawmakers have filed dozens of amendments, with the [Congress matter page](https://www.congressonacional.leg.br/pt_BR/materias/medidas-provisorias/-/mpv/175871){:target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"} tracking the count. Some proposals aim to preserve parts of the regulated market. Reporting suggests the court may not rule until after the October 25 runoff, and the rulings so far have been narrow. One operator won a partial injunction, but it applies only to that company.

For advertisers, one rule controls the ad account, and it is already in effect. Even if Brazilian law changes later, Google would need to update its own policy before these ads could return. Plan for the ban to hold, and treat any reversal as an upside surprise rather than a base case.

## What Every Business Owner Can Learn From This

Even if you never advertise a bet, three lessons apply to any business that buys attention.

### One platform can change your whole funnel overnight

Paid ads are rented traffic. When a policy shifts, campaigns stop, certifications vanish and budget sits idle. Brazil's market felt it almost immediately: one industry demand index fell 20.1 percent week over week after the measure was signed, according to [AGBrief](https://agbrief.com/news/world/29/09/2026/brazils-igaming-demand-drops-20-1-after-nationwide-online-betting-ban/){:target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"}.

### Regulated categories carry extra risk

Gambling, finance, health and similar topics sit in what search professionals call YMYL territory, short for Your Money or Your Life. These categories face stricter rules, more scrutiny and faster enforcement. If your business touches one of them, compliance is part of marketing, not an afterthought. Our guide on [what YMYL means in SEO](https://f9xr.org/articles/2026/09/14/what-is-ymyl-in-seo.html) explains how the standard shapes ranking and ad policy.

### Country rules can override global ones

Google handles gambling country by country. A campaign that is fine in one market can be disallowed in another. If you advertise across borders, check the local policy page and the local law for each market, every time.

## Practical Steps You Can Take This Week

### If you advertised betting, lotteries or horse racing in Brazil

1. **Pause Brazil campaigns for fixed quota betting immediately.** Do not look for workarounds. Policy circumvention can put the whole account at risk.
2. **Read Google's official policy page** and note the exact wording for your category.
3. **Check your certification status** in Google Ads if you run lotteries or horse racing, and re-apply if your certificate was revoked.
4. **Audit landing pages, ad extensions, app listings and video content** that target Brazil.
5. **Talk to a Brazilian gambling and advertising lawyer.** Google's rule and Brazilian law are separate, and both apply.
6. **Tell clients and stakeholders early,** with a clear plan and a revised forecast.

### If you run ads for any other business

1. **Run a policy health check.** Review disapproved ads, restricted categories and account warnings monthly.
2. **Diversify your traffic.** Aim for a healthy mix of organic search, local search, email, social and referrals.

5. **Keep a backup plan.** Know where your budget goes if a campaign type is disallowed tomorrow.
6. **Subscribe to official policy updates** so you hear about changes from the source, not from a competitor.
