For years, the way Indian consumers discovered a quick meal or a new skincare product followed a familiar path: type a query into Google, scroll through results, maybe check a review site, then decide. That path just got a new competitor, and two Indian brands have already jumped in.

KFC India has become the first quick-service restaurant (QSR) brand in the country to advertise on ChatGPT, running a campaign for its Rs 99 Chicken Krisper Meal in partnership with WPP Media. Around the same time, Mamaearth, the personal care brand owned by Honasa Consumer Ltd., became the first Indian brand overall to advertise on the platform, partnering with commerce media company Criteo to buy ad inventory inside ChatGPT conversations.

Gurjot Shah Singh, SVP at EssenceMediacom, announced KFC's move on LinkedIn and called it "the first ads of the post-search era," describing the shift as one "from the search bar to the conversation." That phrase captures exactly why this matters. This is an early signal of where consumer discovery in India, and everywhere else, is headed next.

For business owners, startups, and local brands, this raises an obvious question: if your customers start asking ChatGPT for recommendations instead of typing into Google, will your business even show up in the conversation? What follows is a rundown of exactly what happened, how ChatGPT advertising works in India, and what business owners should be doing right now to prepare. If you're starting from scratch, our earlier guide on [ChatGPT Ads for business owners](https://f9xr.github.io/articles/2026/08/22/chatgpt-ads-guide-business-owners.html) walks through the platform basics in full.

## What Actually Happened: Two Indian Brands Enter ChatGPT Advertising

### KFC India's ChatGPT Campaign

KFC India, working with WPP Media, launched ads on ChatGPT promoting its Rs 99 Chicken Krisper Meal, making it the first QSR brand in the country to advertise on the platform. According to Suhayl Limbada, CMO of KFC India, the move reflects the brand's instinct to keep experimenting with new places to reach consumers, extending the same logic behind its recent collaboration with quick-commerce platform Zepto into the world of conversational AI.

The idea is straightforward but meaningful: someone asking ChatGPT what they can eat for under Rs 100 might now see KFC as part of the answer, appearing as a labeled, sponsored placement below the AI's response.

### Mamaearth's ChatGPT Ad Partnership

Mamaearth, under its parent company Honasa Consumer Ltd., became the first Indian brand overall to advertise on ChatGPT, partnering with Criteo, a commerce media company, to buy ad inventory on the platform. The deal positions Mamaearth as an early mover in using programmatic-style ad buying built specifically for conversational AI environments, rather than traditional search or social advertising. Indian advertising industry outlet [afaqs covered the announcement](https://www.afaqs.com/news/advertising/mamaearth-becomes-first-indian-brand-to-advertise-on-chatgpt-ghazal-alagh-12482403){:target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"} as it broke.

Both campaigns arrive alongside a third entrant, Sebamed, which worked with WPP Media on its own ChatGPT advertising initiative. That brings the early list of Indian ChatGPT advertisers to at least three recognizable consumer brands.

## Why This Is Happening Now: OpenAI's Ad Rollout in India

These campaigns didn't happen in isolation. They're a direct result of OpenAI's broader advertising expansion in India, which unfolded in two clear phases. [Times of India tracked the rollout](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/technology/tech-news/openai-brings-ads-to-chatgpt-in-india-heres-who-will-see-them/articleshow/133556636.cms){:target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"} as it went live, and the timeline below lines up with that reporting.

| Phase | Date | What Happened |
|---|---|---|
| Phase 1 | August 27, 2026 | OpenAI announced managed ads for ChatGPT's Free and Go tier users in India, with WPP and Omnicom as the first official agency partners. Over 50 brands launched campaigns in this initial wave. |
| Phase 2 | September 4, 2026 | OpenAI opened self-serve access to its Ads Manager in India, letting any business, not just the initial agency-backed brands, create and run campaigns directly, with a minimum daily budget of roughly ₹725. |

Ads currently appear only for users on ChatGPT's Free and Go tiers. Users on Plus, Pro, Business, Enterprise, and Education plans remain ad-free, and ads are shown only to users aged 18 and above. That targeting detail matters. Free and Go tier users in India tend to skew toward students, early-career professionals, small business owners, and cost-conscious consumers, a meaningful audience for both consumer brands and budget-friendly B2B services.

OpenAI has been clear that ads appear as separate, labeled placements below ChatGPT's generated response, and that the AI's actual answer is not influenced by advertising. As the company puts it, "ChatGPT responses are generated independently. Ads are not integrated into responses," and ad placements do not represent an endorsement or recommendation from OpenAI itself. The official [Ads in ChatGPT documentation](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001047-ads-in-chatgpt){:target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"} spells out the same commitments in more detail.

## Why Conversational Ads Are Fundamentally Different

A traditional Google search for "KFC near me" tells an advertiser what someone typed. A conversation with ChatGPT can reveal a lot more: what the person actually needs, what constraints they're working with (budget, dietary preference, location), and what decision they're trying to make in that exact moment.

For example, someone might ask ChatGPT "what are the best affordable meal options for a quick dinner tonight," and receive a generated response followed by a relevant sponsored placement. That is a meaningfully different discovery moment than scanning ten blue links, because the AI has already done the work of understanding intent before the ad even appears.

This is the shift Singh described when he called it the move "from the search bar to the conversation." Food decisions, and really most purchase decisions, have always happened through some form of internal or social conversation: what sounds good tonight, what's worth trying, what fits the budget. AI platforms like ChatGPT are now becoming part of that conversation directly, rather than just an information lookup tool.

## What ChatGPT Advertising Means for Business Owners and Startups

Beyond the headline, this rollout changes the picture for businesses that have never touched AI advertising. Four angles deserve attention now.

### 1. Early Movers Get an Advantage

With more than 50 brands expected to onboard during India's initial commercial rollout, the door is open, but it won't stay uncrowded for long. Brands that test ChatGPT advertising now have a chance to learn the platform's dynamics, audience behavior, and creative approach before the space gets as saturated as search and social advertising eventually became.

### 2. Small Businesses Can Test This Too

While KFC and Mamaearth are large, well-known names, OpenAI's self-serve Ads Manager, live since September 4, 2026, lowers the barrier to entry considerably. With a minimum daily budget of around ₹725, small businesses and startups can realistically test conversational advertising without needing an agency partnership or a massive marketing budget.

### 3. Being Recommended by AI Matters More Than Ever

This development sits alongside a much bigger shift happening across search and AI discovery in general. Even without running a paid ad, how frequently and positively your business is mentioned across the internet increasingly shapes whether AI tools like ChatGPT, Gemini, or Perplexity recommend you organically in a conversation. Paid advertising on these platforms is one lever, but organic visibility and brand reputation remain just as important. Our guide on [key strategies to rank on AI](https://f9xr.github.io/articles/2026/09/07/key-strategies-to-rank-on-ai.html) covers the mechanics behind that organic side in depth.

### 4. Local Businesses Should Watch This Space Closely

If you run a local restaurant, service business, or retail store, this shift matters even if you're not ready to advertise on ChatGPT yourself. As more consumers start asking AI assistants for local recommendations, the businesses that show up, whether through paid placement or strong organic AI visibility, will hold a clear discovery advantage over those that don't. The signals that help AI tools [pick local businesses](https://f9xr.github.io/articles/2026/08/16/how-ai-picks-local-businesses-2026.html) apply to this channel too.

## Practical Tips for Getting Ahead of ChatGPT Advertising

You don't need a big campaign budget to start preparing. These moves are low-cost, and they compound.

1. Start with a small test campaign. The self-serve Ads Manager's low minimum daily budget makes testing low-risk, and it tells you how conversational advertising actually performs for your specific audience.
2. Make your business part of the answer, not just the ad. Strengthen your organic presence so AI models are more likely to mention your business naturally in relevant conversations. Structuring pages around the questions people ask, the way this [AEO landing page guide](https://f9xr.github.io/articles/2026/08/23/aeo-landing-page-optimization-guide.html) explains, gives AI models clean material to work with.
3. Keep your business information consistent everywhere. AI systems pull signals from across the internet, so consistent business details across your website, listings, and social profiles matter just as much for AI visibility as they do for traditional local SEO.
4. Watch how your competitors respond. If brands in your industry start testing ChatGPT ads, monitor how their campaigns perform and adjust your own strategy accordingly rather than waiting until the space becomes crowded.
5. Don't abandon traditional SEO in the process. Conversational AI advertising is a new channel, not a replacement for the search visibility and website optimization that still drives a large share of discovery today. Making your [site visible to AI engines](https://f9xr.github.io/articles/2026/08/14/steps-make-business-site-visible-to-ai.html) works alongside classic SEO, not instead of it.
