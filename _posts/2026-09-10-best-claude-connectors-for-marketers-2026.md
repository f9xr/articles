## What Are Claude Connectors, Exactly?

Claude connectors are prebuilt integrations that link Claude to outside services such as Gmail, Slack, Notion, HubSpot, and hundreds more. They run on the [Model Context Protocol](https://www.anthropic.com/news/model-context-protocol), an open standard that Anthropic and others sometimes describe as a kind of universal plug for AI tools. You authenticate once, usually through the same "Sign in with Google" style OAuth flow you already use everywhere else, and Claude inherits your existing permissions inside that app.

Under the hood, most connectors use either OAuth, the same authorization standard you use when you click "Sign in with Google" on a third party app, or an API key you generate inside the source app and paste into Claude. Consumer tools like Gmail, Calendar, and Notion tend to use the simple OAuth flow. Developer or data heavy tools like Snowflake or Supabase usually need an API key.

The directory has grown fast. The Claude Connectors Directory hosts over 414 integrations across 30 categories, and that number keeps climbing every month as new partners join.

## The Best Claude Connectors for Marketers and Business Owners

Here are the connectors that genuinely change day to day work for marketing teams, agencies, and small business owners, based on what they do rather than how flashy they sound.

### 1. Google Workspace (Gmail, Calendar, Drive)

This is the obvious starting point for almost every business. Google Workspace connectors for Gmail, Google Calendar, and Google Drive let you search and read emails using natural language queries, send and reply to messages, and search, retrieve, share, or move files in Drive. By default Claude asks before it sends anything on your behalf, so you stay in control.

Practical use: ask Claude to summarise every client email from the past week, draft replies in your tone, or pull the latest version of a proposal straight from Drive without digging through folders. If you are working on [making your business site visible to AI](https://f9xr.org/articles/2026/08/14/steps-make-business-site-visible-to-ai.html), having your Google Workspace connected means Claude can reference your actual business documents when crafting responses. Teams focused on [key strategies to rank on AI](https://f9xr.org/articles/2026/09/07/key-strategies-to-rank-on-ai.html) will find this especially useful for feeding Claude real business context.

### 2. Slack

If your team lives in Slack, this connector saves real time. Claude can search channel history, summarise a long thread you missed, or draft an announcement message. It is one of the connectors that can also render live interfaces inside your chat, so you can actually see and interact with Slack content without switching tabs.

### 3. Notion

Marketing teams tend to keep content calendars, campaign notes, and briefs in Notion. Connect it and Claude can pull an existing brief, check what is scheduled this week, or draft a new page based on a conversation you just had. It saves the constant back and forth of copying content in and out of documents.

### 4. HubSpot or Salesforce (CRM data)

This is where connectors start to feel less like a chat toy and more like a business tool. With your CRM connected, you can ask Claude to summarise a lead's history before a call, draft a follow up email based on the deal stage, or flag deals that have gone quiet. For a small business owner who is also the salesperson, this cuts a lot of manual lookup time.

### 5. Semrush

Semrush shipped an [official Claude connector](https://www.semrush.com/news/469301-semrush-launches-official-connector-for-claude-bringing-marketing-intelligence-into-ai-conversations/) recently and it is a big one for anyone doing SEO or content marketing. Inside Claude you can query billions of keywords and trillions of backlinks, run competitor gap analysis, build content briefs, and pull market research such as traffic sources and audience demographics, without exporting a single CSV. If you write blog content or run local SEO campaigns, this connector alone can replace a lot of tab switching between Claude and a separate SEO dashboard. Marketers who [monitor AI search trends](https://f9xr.org/articles/2026/08/26/monitor-ai-search-trends-boost-visibility-citations.html) will find Semrush data especially valuable for tracking how AI engines cite and surface your content.

### 6. Windsor.ai (marketing data aggregation)

If you run ads across several platforms, Windsor.ai is worth knowing about. It pulls marketing data from over 345 sources into a single Claude conversation, so instead of exporting reports from Meta Ads, Google Ads, and email marketing separately, you can ask one question and get a blended answer.

### 7. Canva or Figma

For businesses that produce their own social graphics or landing pages, having Canva or Figma connected means Claude can help edit a design, pull assets, or check a file's status directly inside the chat window, rather than you explaining a design brief from scratch every time.

### 8. WordPress.com

If your website or blog runs on WordPress.com, this connector lets Claude read and manage posts, pages, and comments directly. It can display your most recent blog posts, locate articles on a specific topic, summarise site statistics for the month, or check user roles and permissions, all through secure OAuth authentication so Claude only touches what you approve.

### 9. Stripe

For business owners who want quick visibility into revenue without opening a separate dashboard, the Stripe connector exposes payment and revenue data directly to Claude, so you can ask plain questions like "how did this month compare to last month" and get an answer sourced from your actual account.

### 10. Meeting notetakers (Zoom, Fireflies, Fathom, Read AI)

If you are on client calls constantly, connecting a meeting notetaker means Claude can pull transcripts and summaries automatically. Instead of writing recap emails by hand after every call, you can ask Claude to draft the follow up based on what was actually said.

## How to Set Up a Claude Connector (Step by Step)

1. Open Claude on desktop or the Claude app. Desktop tends to have a smoother setup flow than mobile.
2. Click the toolbox or "+" icon near the chat box, then choose Customize, then Connectors.
3. Browse the Connectors Directory by category, or search for the specific app you want.
4. Click Connect on the tool you need and sign in the same way you normally would for that app.
5. Approve the requested permissions. Most connectors only ask for what they actually need to function.
6. Start a normal conversation and mention the connected app naturally. Claude will pull in the relevant tool automatically.

Most consumer connectors take about three minutes to set up. Enterprise tools that need admin approval will take a bit longer.

## Security and Privacy Tips for Business Owners

* Only connect accounts you are comfortable with Claude reading. You can disconnect at any time from Customize, then Connectors.
* Review what each connector can actually do before connecting. The permissions are listed clearly, and most sending or posting actions require your approval by default.
* If you run a team, decide early who gets to enable which connectors. On Team and Enterprise plans, an owner has to turn certain connectors on organisation wide before members can use them.
* Do not connect financial tools like Stripe on a shared device or a device you do not fully control.
* If you have ten or more connectors active, switch the tool access setting to "On demand" so your conversations run a little leaner.
