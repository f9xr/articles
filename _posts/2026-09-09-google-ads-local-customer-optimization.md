## What Is Google Ads Local Customer Optimization?

Local Customer Optimization is a new campaign-level setting inside Performance Max campaigns for store goals. In simple terms, it helps Google prioritize showing your ads to people who are nearby and appear ready to take action, whether that's visiting your store, requesting directions, or making contact.

According to [Google's help documentation](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/12971048?hl=en){:target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"}, the feature draws on signals from people who are actively:

* Navigating toward a location
* Planning a trip or errand
* Searching for a business or service in a specific area

These signals are pulled from across Google Maps, Waze, and local ad formats within Google Search, meaning your campaign can reach potential customers at the exact moment they're deciding where to go, not just when they're casually browsing.

### How It Fits Into Performance Max

Performance Max is Google's automated campaign type that shows your ads across Google's entire network, Search, Maps, YouTube, Gmail, and Display, all from a single campaign. Local Customer Optimization is essentially a refinement layer on top of that, specifically for businesses whose goal is driving people to a physical location rather than an online purchase.

This update is also connected to a bigger transition Google announced recently: the migration of Local Service Ads into the main Google Ads platform. Coverage like [Search Engine Land's breakdown](https://searchengineland.com/google-adds-local-customer-optimization-to-performance-max-484785){:target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"} shows Local Customer Optimization is part of where that migration is heading, giving local-focused campaigns their own dedicated optimization path within Performance Max.

## How to Turn On Local Customer Optimization

Setting this up isn't complicated, but there are a few specific steps and requirements worth knowing before you get started.

### For a New Campaign

1. Go to **Campaigns** in your Google Ads account
2. Select **+ New Campaign**
3. Choose **Local store visits and promotions** as your campaign objective
4. Select **Performance Max** as the campaign type
5. In the **Local Customer Optimization** panel, toggle the feature on
6. Select the business locations you want to target, either from your Business Profile account or your linked location assets
7. Continue through the rest of your campaign setup as usual

### For an Existing Campaign

If you already have an eligible Performance Max campaign running for store goals, you can:

1. Go to **Campaigns**
2. Select the campaign you want to edit
3. Find the **Local Customer Optimization** toggle within your budget and bidding optimization settings
4. Turn it on

## Important Limitations to Know Before You Enable It

This feature isn't compatible with every type of campaign, so check your setup before assuming it's available to you.

| Limitation | What It Means |
|---|---|
| Not compatible with online-only goals | Local Customer Optimization only works for campaigns focused on offline, in-store objectives |
| Not compatible with Merchant Center feeds | If your Performance Max campaign advertises products from a linked Google Merchant Center account, this feature can't be enabled |
| Requires a separate eligible campaign | Retailers using feed-based Performance Max campaigns will need to set up a separate campaign specifically for local store goals |
| Business location required | You must select and link your business locations through your Business Profile or linked location assets |

If you're a retailer running feed-based Performance Max campaigns tied to Merchant Center, you won't be able to simply flip this toggle on inside your existing setup. You'll need a dedicated campaign built specifically around store visit goals.

## Practical Tips for Local Businesses Using This Feature

1. **Make sure your [Business Profile](https://f9xr.org/articles/2026/08/16/zero-click-search-google-business-profile-homepage.html) is fully updated first.** Local Customer Optimization pulls location data from your linked [Business Profile or location assets](https://f9xr.org/services/google-business-optimization.html), so outdated hours, addresses, or contact details will directly affect campaign accuracy.
2. **Set up a dedicated campaign if you use Merchant Center.** Don't assume you can toggle this on inside an existing feed-based campaign. Build a separate Performance Max campaign specifically for store visit goals if that applies to you.
3. **Enable it early, not during peak holiday week.** Performance Max campaigns typically need time to gather data and optimize. Turning this on weeks before the holiday rush gives Google's algorithm more runway to learn and perform well when it matters most.
4. **Pair it with Store Sales measurement if available.** If you're able to upload transaction data, combining Local Customer Optimization with Store Sales reporting gives you a much clearer view of actual ROI, not just clicks or impressions.
5. **Double check your targeted locations.** Since this feature relies on selecting specific business locations, review your list carefully, especially if you operate multiple storefronts, to make sure every eligible location is included.
