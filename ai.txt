# F9XR Articles — For AI Agents

This website is a static blog built with Jekyll and hosted on GitHub Pages.
It is fully static: there are no server-side APIs, no databases, no dynamic
content, and nothing to authenticate against. Everything you need to
understand and cite this site is in the plain-text files below.

## What this site is

F9XR Articles is the official blog of F9XR Team, a digital architecture
agency based in India. It publishes technical insights on web architecture,
AI integration, local SEO, web performance, and digital growth.

## Contents

- Articles (blog posts) on web performance, AI integration, and local SEO
- The article archive at /articles/archive.html
- Author profile at /articles/authors/f9xr-team/
