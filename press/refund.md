## Reading Is Free

F9XR Articles is a free publication. You are never charged to read, share, or browse any article on this site, so nothing here requires a refund.

## Sponsored Content and Collaborations

If you engage F9XR Team for sponsored content, a press release, or a placement on the F9XR publishing network, the commercial terms — including any eligibility for refunds or re-runs — are set out in the written agreement you receive before purchase. If you believe you qualify for a refund on such a service, contact us at [{{ site.email }}](mailto:{{ site.email }}) within **7 days** of purchase with your invoice number and the reason, and we will review it on those agreed terms.

## F9XR Team Services

F9XR Articles does not itself sell website development, local SEO, or other agency services. Those services are sold by F9XR Team through its main site and are governed by the [F9XR refund and cancellation policy](https://f9xr.github.io/legals/refund.html){:target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"}.

## Corrections Instead of Refunds

Because this site is editorial, the remedy for an error in an article is a correction, not a refund. See our [correction policy]({{ '/press/correction-policy.html' | relative_url }}) for how we handle errors, and our [editorial policy]({{ '/press/editorial-policy.html' | relative_url }}) for our content review cadence.
