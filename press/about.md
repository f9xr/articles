F9XR Articles is the publishing platform of F9XR, a digital architecture agency based in India with a global client footprint. We publish practical, technically grounded content on web architecture, AI integration, local SEO, and high-performance digital systems — the same work we do for clients every day.

## What We Cover

F9XR Articles focuses on four core areas:

- **Web Architecture** — Performance optimization, Core Web Vitals, mobile-first design, and the technical infrastructure that makes websites convert.
- **AI Integration** — Practical applications of AI tools for business workflows, content strategy, search optimization, and automation.
- **Local SEO** — Google Business Profile optimization, local pack ranking, citation management, and the systems that drive measurable local visibility.
- **Digital Systems** — The operational layer — analytics, monitoring, security, maintenance, and the processes that keep digital properties running reliably.

## Who Writes Here

Every article on F9XR Articles is written by members of the F9XR Team who have direct, hands-on experience in the subject matter. We don't outsource content creation. The same people building high-performance web ecosystems and managing local SEO infrastructure for clients are the ones writing about how these systems work in practice.

Our [editorial process]({{ '/press/editorial-policy.html' | relative_url }}), [ethics standards]({{ '/press/ethics-policy.html' | relative_url }}), and [correction policy]({{ '/press/correction-policy.html' | relative_url }}) ensure that everything we publish meets the same technical bar we apply to client work.

## Why We Publish

Most digital marketing content is written by people who have never managed a production server, debugged a Core Web Vitals regression, or tracked a local pack ranking through three consecutive algorithm updates. We publish because the businesses we work with — and the broader community of developers, marketers, and founders — deserve content written from direct operational experience, not secondhand summaries.

## Our Principles

1. **Technical accuracy over clickbait.** We verify claims against official documentation and live testing. If we say something works, we can show you how we tested it.
2. **Actionable over theoretical.** Every article includes practical steps, real examples, or working code — not just concepts.
3. **Current and maintained.** Published articles are reviewed on a regular cadence. Outdated information gets flagged, updated, or removed. See our [editorial policy]({{ '/press/editorial-policy.html' | relative_url }}) for the full review schedule.
4. **Transparent about AI.** When AI tools assist in creating content, we disclose it. Our [ethics policy]({{ '/press/ethics-policy.html' | relative_url }}) explains how we handle AI-assisted content.
