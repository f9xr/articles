F9XR Articles operates under a set of ethical publishing standards designed to ensure that every piece of content is accurate, independently verifiable, and free from undisclosed conflicts of interest. This policy applies to all content published under the F9XR Articles brand.

## Data Integrity Standards

F9XR Articles applies the following standards to all data, claims, and assertions published on the platform:

### Verification Before Publication
- **Technical claims must be tested.** Any claim about performance, behavior, compatibility, or capability is verified through direct testing or reference to official documentation before publication.
- **Benchmarks must be reproducible.** Performance benchmarks include environment specifications (hardware, software versions, configuration) sufficient for independent reproduction.
- **Statistics must be sourced.** Quantitative claims cite their origin. When data is derived from proprietary testing, the methodology is disclosed.

### Accuracy in Code Examples
- **All code blocks are executed.** Every code example published on F9XR Articles is tested in a controlled environment to confirm that it produces the stated result.
- **Environment details are documented.** When behavior varies by environment (OS, runtime version, configuration), the tested environment is noted in the article.
- **Dependencies are pinned.** Code examples that depend on external libraries specify minimum compatible versions to prevent breakage from upstream changes.

### Correction of Errors
When inaccuracies are identified — whether by readers, team members, or automated monitoring — they are resolved through the process described in the [F9XR Correction Policy]({{ '/press/correction-policy/' | relative_url }}).

## Conflict of Interest Management

F9XR Articles maintains the following policies to prevent undisclosed conflicts of interest from influencing published content:

### Independence from Vendors
- Content recommendations are based on technical merit, not commercial relationships.
- When the F9XR Team has a commercial relationship with a vendor whose product is discussed, that relationship is disclosed in the article.
- Sponsored content is clearly labeled and separated from editorial content.

### No Pay-for-Placement
- F9XR Articles does not accept payment for positive reviews, favorable rankings, or inclusion in comparison articles.
- Product mentions in editorial content are determined solely by relevance to the reader and technical merit.
- Affiliate relationships, where they exist, are disclosed in the article and do not influence editorial judgment.

### Personal Interest Disclosure
- Team members with financial interests, advisory roles, or personal relationships with entities discussed in published content must disclose those interests to the editorial team before publication.
- The editorial team reserves the right to assign review of such content to a team member without the disclosed interest.

## Technical Claim Verification

F9XR Articles applies a structured verification process to technical claims:

### Claim Categories

| Category | Verification Method |
|---|---|
| API behavior | Direct testing against current documentation |
| Performance benchmarks | Reproducible testing with disclosed methodology |
| Security claims | Reference to CVE databases, vendor advisories, or independent audits |
| Compatibility claims | Testing across stated platforms and versions |
| Best practices | Alignment with official vendor guidance and industry consensus |

### Third-Party Code and Libraries
- Code examples that depend on third-party libraries are tested against the latest stable release at the time of publication.
- Known vulnerabilities in referenced dependencies are noted where material to the article's recommendations.
- When a recommended library has known security concerns, those concerns are disclosed alongside the recommendation.

## Editorial Independence

F9XR Articles maintains editorial independence from external influences:

- **No advertiser influence.** Advertising relationships do not affect editorial decisions, article placement, or content recommendations.
- **No political or ideological bias.** Technical content is evaluated on its merits, not on alignment with any political, ideological, or commercial agenda.
- **Reader-first commitment.** When a decision conflicts with commercial interests, the reader's interest takes precedence.

## Accountability

F9XR Articles holds itself accountable to the standards described in this policy through:

- **Transparent ownership.** The organizational structure and funding model are publicly documented in the [Ownership, Funding, & Transparency]({{ '/press/transparency/' | relative_url }}) page.
- **Open corrections.** The correction process is public and documented in the [F9XR Correction Policy]({{ '/press/correction-policy/' | relative_url }}).
- **Open source.** The source code for this publication is publicly available at [github.com/f9xr/articles](https://github.com/f9xr/articles), enabling independent review of publishing practices.
