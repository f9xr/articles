F9XR Articles is published by the F9XR Team. This page discloses the organizational structure, funding model, and any commercial relationships that may be relevant to readers evaluating the content published on this platform.

## Organizational Ownership

F9XR Articles is owned and operated by the F9XR Team, a digital architecture agency based in India. The F9XR Team is responsible for all editorial decisions, content production, and technical operations related to this publication.

- **Legal entity:** F9XR Team
- **Location:** India
- **Website:** [f9xr.github.io](https://f9xr.github.io)
- **Contact:** [tontufytservices@gmail.com](mailto:tontufytservices@gmail.com)

The F9XR Team is not a subsidiary of, nor does it receive editorial direction from, any external organization. All content decisions are made internally by the editorial team.

## Funding Model

F9XR Articles is funded through the following revenue sources:

### Primary Revenue
- **Client services.** The F9XR Team generates revenue through web development, SEO consulting, and digital architecture services provided to clients. These services are the primary funding source for the publication.
- **Product and tool development.** Revenue from tools and digital products developed by the F9XR Team.

### Secondary Revenue
- **Affiliate relationships.** F9XR Articles may earn commissions from affiliate links to third-party products or services. These links are clearly labeled within the content where they appear. Affiliate relationships do not influence editorial recommendations.
- **Sponsored content.** F9XR Articles may accept sponsored content from third parties. All sponsored content is clearly labeled as such and is subject to the same editorial review process described in the [F9XR Editorial Policy]({{ '/press/editorial-policy/' | relative_url }}).

### Revenue We Do Not Accept
- **Pay-for-placement.** F9XR Articles does not accept payment for positive reviews, favorable rankings, or inclusion in editorial content.
- **Undisclosed compensation.** No team member accepts compensation from third parties in exchange for favorable coverage without prior disclosure to the editorial team and readers.

## Sponsored Content Disclosure

When content is sponsored by a third party, the following disclosures are applied:

- **Visible label.** A clearly visible notice appears at the top of the article stating that the content was sponsored, identifying the sponsor, and linking to this transparency page.
- **Editorial control.** Sponsored content is subject to the same editorial review, fact-checking, and accuracy standards as all other content published on F9XR Articles. Sponsors do not have veto power over editorial decisions.
- **Reader-first commitment.** Sponsored content must provide genuine value to the reader. Content that exists solely to promote a sponsor without delivering technical value is not published.

## Affiliate Relationships

F9XR Articles may include affiliate links to third-party products, services, or tools. When affiliate links are present:

- The article includes a disclosure statement indicating that the link is an affiliate link.
- The editorial recommendation is based on the same technical merit standards applied to non-affiliate content.
- Affiliate relationships do not influence the ranking, placement, or tone of product comparisons or recommendations.

## Team Independence

Members of the F9XR Team may have personal interests, investments, or advisory relationships with companies or products discussed in published content. The following standards apply:

- **Disclosure requirement.** Team members must disclose relevant interests to the editorial team before contributing to content that discusses the related entity.
- **Recusal.** When a team member has a material financial interest in a company discussed in an article, that team member recuses themselves from the editorial review of that article.
- **Transparency.** Material interests are disclosed in the relevant article.

## Advertising

F9XR Articles may display third-party advertisements. Advertising content is:

- Clearly separated from editorial content.
- Not allowed to influence article placement, rankings, or editorial recommendations.
- Subject to the advertising policies of the hosting platform (GitHub Pages).

## Changes to This Policy

This transparency page is updated whenever there are material changes to the ownership, funding model, or commercial relationships of F9XR Articles. The date of the last update is noted at the bottom of this page.
