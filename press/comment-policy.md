F9XR Articles uses comments to extend the conversation beyond the article itself. The comment section is a place for readers to ask technical questions, share first-hand experience, correct an error with evidence, and challenge an idea without attacking the person behind it.

This policy explains how comments work, what is welcome, what is removed, and how moderation decisions can be reviewed.

## {% include lucide.html name="message-square" style="color:#3b82f6; margin-right:0.4em;" %}How Comments Work

Comments are powered by [Utterances](https://utteranc.es/), which stores each discussion as a public GitHub issue in the [f9xr/articles repository](https://github.com/f9xr/articles){:target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"}. A free GitHub account is required to comment.

Because comments are public GitHub issues:

- Your GitHub username and public profile are visible with every comment.
- Comments may be indexed by search engines and can remain available after an article is updated.
- GitHub's [Terms of Service](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/github-terms/github-terms-of-service){:target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"} and [Community Guidelines](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/github-terms/github-community-guidelines){:target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"} apply in addition to this policy.
- Comments are not private messages. Do not include passwords, API keys, customer data, or other confidential information.

## {% include lucide.html name="heart-handshake" style="color:#3b82f6; margin-right:0.4em;" %}What Is Welcome

- **Technical questions.** If something in an article is unclear, ask. Include the version, environment, and the exact behavior you are seeing.
- **First-hand experience.** Reports from people who have shipped, tested, or operated the systems discussed in an article are valuable.
- **Evidence-based corrections.** If a claim is wrong or outdated, point to the relevant documentation, reproduction steps, or benchmark. See the [Correction Policy]({{ '/press/correction-policy.html' | relative_url }}) for the formal process.
- **Constructive disagreement.** Challenge the reasoning, not the author. Explain what you would do differently and why.
- **Useful links.** Link to primary sources, maintained documentation, or reproducible examples. Briefly explain why the link is relevant.

## {% include lucide.html name="ban" style="color:#3b82f6; margin-right:0.4em;" %}What Is Not Allowed

The following content may be removed or hidden, usually without prior notice:

- **Spam and repetitive promotion.** Unsolicited advertising, referral links, lead-generation pitches, and the same link posted across multiple articles.
- **Harassment and personal attacks.** Insults, threats, intimidation, sustained hostility, or attacks based on identity, background, or belief.
- **Hate speech and discrimination.** Content that promotes or encourages violence, exclusion, or hatred against a protected group.
- **Doxxing and privacy violations.** Sharing another person's private information, including home address, phone number, employer details, or private communications, without consent.
- **Impersonation.** Pretending to be an F9XR team member, an author, or another commenter.
- **Deliberate misinformation.** Fabricated claims, manipulated data, or misleading technical advice presented as verified fact.
- **Malware, piracy, and illegal content.** Links to malicious software, credential-stealing tools, pirated material, or instructions for illegal activity.
- **Off-topic flooding.** Long threads that have no connection to the article, repeated comments, or automated posting.
- **AI-generated spam.** Low-effort, machine-generated comments posted at volume, including comments that only restate the article or contain generic praise with a promotional link.

## {% include lucide.html name="scale" style="color:#3b82f6; margin-right:0.4em;" %}How Moderation Works

Comments are reviewed as time allows. Moderation is not a guarantee that every comment will be read before it appears. The team may take any of the following actions:

| Situation | Action |
|---|---|
| Minor formatting or relevance issue | Edit or hide the comment with a note |
| Spam, promotion, or off-topic flooding | Delete the comment and block repeat posting |
| Harassment, hate speech, or doxxing | Delete the comment, lock the thread, and report to GitHub when appropriate |
| Credible security or legal concern | Remove the comment and preserve relevant details for review |
| Repeated violations | Restrict the account from commenting on the repository |

Moderation decisions are based on the content of the comment and the context of the discussion. Criticism of F9XR Articles, its authors, or its commercial services is not, by itself, a violation. A comment is not removed because it is negative.

## {% include lucide.html name="calendar-check" style="color:#3b82f6; margin-right:0.4em;" %}Response Times

- **Abuse reports:** reviewed within 24 hours when possible.
- **Moderation appeals:** acknowledged within 3 business days.
- **Technical corrections:** handled under the timelines in the [Correction Policy]({{ '/press/correction-policy.html' | relative_url }}).

## {% include lucide.html name="flag" style="color:#3b82f6; margin-right:0.4em;" %}Reporting a Comment

If you see a comment that violates this policy, report it through one of these channels:

1. **GitHub:** Use the report option on the comment or open a private report through GitHub's [abuse reporting process](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse){:target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"}.
2. **Email:** Send the article URL, a link to the comment, and a short explanation to [hello@f9xr.org](mailto:hello@f9xr.org) with the subject line "Comment Report".

Do not reply to abusive comments with more abuse. Report the comment and move on.

## {% include lucide.html name="lock" style="color:#3b82f6; margin-right:0.4em;" %}Privacy

Comments are public. Anything you post in a comment can be read, copied, and indexed by others. For private matters, including security reports, billing questions, and personal data requests, use email instead of the comment section.

The site's general data practices are described in the [Privacy Policy]({{ '/press/privacy.html' | relative_url }}). GitHub's handling of issue data is governed by GitHub's own privacy statement.

## {% include lucide.html name="life-buoy" style="color:#3b82f6; margin-right:0.4em;" %}Appeals

If you believe a moderation decision was made in error, email [hello@f9xr.org](mailto:hello@f9xr.org) with the subject line "Comment Moderation Appeal". Include:

- The article URL.
- The comment text or a link to the issue.
- The reason you believe the decision should be reconsidered.

Appeals are reviewed by a team member who was not involved in the original decision when possible. The outcome is final, but a successful appeal will restore the comment or clarify the rule that applied.

## {% include lucide.html name="scroll-text" style="color:#3b82f6; margin-right:0.4em;" %}Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as the comment system, the community, or legal requirements change. The revision date at the top of this page reflects the most recent material change. Continued use of the comment section after an update means you accept the revised policy.
