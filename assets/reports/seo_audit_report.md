## Project Overview

F9XR Articles is the official blog of the F9XR Team, a digital architecture agency based in India. The site is static HTML generated by Jekyll, hosted on GitHub Pages. Content consists of technical articles covering web architecture, AI integration, local SEO, web performance, and digital growth strategies. The 120-post corpus (published Jul 2026–Oct 2026) follows consistent front-matter conventions and uses FAQPage schema. Recent editorial output includes the 2026-10-17 Meta Commercial Terms article.

## Executive Summary

The site has solid foundational SEO: consistent canonicalization, FAQPage/BlogPosting schema, sized images, robots.txt and sitemap generated by `jekyll-sitemap`, feeds, llms.txt/ai.txt/humans.txt/security.txt present, and agent-discovery assets under `.well-known/`. No major crawl-blocking issues.

A complete evidence sweep (2026-10-17) was performed against the expanded skill criteria. Code-level issues identified and fixed: two incorrect internal links (404s), stale report exclude paths (content leak risk), missing `dateModified` on 3 posts, missing sitewide entity graph causing dangling `@id` references, publisher Organization not consistently @id-linked, no explicit AI crawler governance, and Eonads advertising disclosure/accuracy (content fix applied).

High-value remaining gaps require owner/editorial decisions: NAP (real address/phone), legal entity name/address, named bylines for legacy posts, `reviewed_by` field fully populated (scaffolding added), and formalizing ad-network disclosure across policy pages. No indexation-blocking defects remain.
