## Recommendations

1. **Title CTR testing:** Experiment with clearer benefit statements on high-traffic posts.
2. **Freshness:** Keep updating cornerstone posts when policy/terms change (e.g., AI crawler policies).
3. **Image captions:** Optional, not required; current approach is fine.
4. **Consistency:** Maintain dateModified when editing posts.

## Verdict

**Eligible for Google Discover.** No technical blockers; focus on CTR and freshness.
